# SNK Neo Geo Pocket / Color

| | |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Neo Geo Pocket (1998, monochrome), **Neo Geo Pocket Color** (1999) |
| **Lifespan** | Oct 1998 – 2001; ~2M units sold |
| **Library size** | ~82 titles across both models |
| **Backward compat** | NGPC plays monochrome NGP cartridges. That is BC, **not** another platform. |
| **Last verified** | 2026-07-29 |
| **Coverage** | Near-complete. The library is small enough to cover properly. |

## Summary

| | Count |
|---|---|
| **Debut games** | **17** |
| of which still `Stranded` | 6 |
| of which since `Ported` | 11 |
| **Contested** | 0 |

**Why the Neo Geo Pocket strands games.** Very little, and for a specific reason:
**SNK's 2020–21 reissue programme was complete.** Roughly a dozen NGPC
titles were released individually on Switch, plus the *Neo Geo Pocket Color
Selection Vol. 1 and 2* compilations, an thorough rescue of a handheld
that sold 2 million units and died in under three years.

What remains stranded is stranded because SNK **does not own it alone**:

1. **The Capcom crossovers.** `SNK vs. Capcom: Card Fighters' Clash` needs Capcom's
 roster rights. Its sibling `Match of the Millennium` was reissued because Capcom
 agreed; the card game was not.
2. **`Sonic the Hedgehog Pocket Adventure`** needs Sega, an SNK-developed Sonic
 game is a licensing arrangement neither company has renewed.
3. **Japan-only oddities** with no series behind them.

| Pac-Man, Puyo Pop, Bust-A-Move Pocket | 1999–2000 | Conversions of games that debuted on earlier home platforms. |
| Any *NGPC Selection* / Switch reissue | 2020–21 | Re-releases by definition; they are what moved most of this library to `Ported`. |
| *(games with an earlier arcade release)* | | **Not excluded.** Arcades are out of scope; the first *home* release counts. |

## Appendix: the tail

- **Japan-only releases (~30 titles).** Mahjong, pachinko, `Dive Alert` variants and
 licensed tie-ins. Mostly stranded, but low-value.
- **Monochrome NGP titles (~12).** Superseded within a year by colour versions of the
 same games; the mono originals were skipped by the reissue programme.

## Coverage gaps

- The Japan-only tail is characterised, not enumerated.
- SNK's Switch reissue programme could still expand; `Ported` may grow.
