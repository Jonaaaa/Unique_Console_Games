## ⚠ A modern handheld with a crank

The Playdate is a yellow 1-bit handheld with a **mechanical crank** on its side as a
primary input. Panic, better known as a Mac software developer and the publisher of
`Firewatch` and `Untitled Goose Game`, designed it as a deliberately small, complete
object, and distributed its first two seasons of games as a "Season" subscription
delivered weekly over Wi-Fi.

Its stranding profile is unusual: games are stranded **by the crank**, which nothing
else has, and by a 1-bit monochrome display that is an aesthetic choice rather than a
limitation.

**Why the Playdate strands games.** The crank, mainly. A game whose core verb is
"turn a physical handle at a controlled speed" has no equivalent input elsewhere, and
several Season 1 titles are built entirely around it. Beyond that, Panic is a small
publisher with no incentive to port, and the Season games were commissioned works
rather than independently owned properties.
