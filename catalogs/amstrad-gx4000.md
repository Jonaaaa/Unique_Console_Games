## ⚠ Nearly empty, by rule

The GX4000 was **an Amstrad CPC Plus computer without the keyboard**, launched in 1990
against the Mega Drive. Its 27 cartridges were almost entirely conversions of existing
CPC floppy and tape games, so under this repo's rules — a prior release on a home
computer disqualifies, on the same principle as PC-first titles — nearly the whole
library fails the debut test.

Amstrad discontinued it within months. It is the **third platform here where the
majority of the library fails the debut test** rather than merely being uncatalogued,
alongside the [Atari 5200](atari-5200.md) and [Amiga CD32](amiga-cd32.md) — all three
being home computers in console shells.

**Why the GX4000 strands games.** Its few genuine debuts are stranded because Amstrad
left gaming, the console sold ~15,000 units in Europe only, and the developers involved
were small British studios that mostly folded during the early-90s consolidation.

## Coverage gaps

- Complete for identifiable debuts, but CPC/GX4000 release ordering in 1990 is thinly
  documented and one or two of the exclusions could be argued the other way.
- Amstrad's rapid exit means little paperwork survives to settle them.
