| **Coverage** | Notable titles verified individually. | ## ⚠ A phone that was also a handheld

The N-Gage was a Symbian mobile phone sold as a game system, with games on MMC
cartridges. It sold poorly — the original required removing the battery to change
games, and you held it sideways to make calls, which was mocked at the time.

It is included here because it took removable game media, had a dedicated games
library, and was marketed as a Game Boy Advance competitor. Its stranding profile is
unusual: **Nokia exited gaming entirely**, so nothing has any owner with an interest,
and several titles were online-dependent on services that no longer exist.

- **Franchise conversions (~40 titles).** The majority of the library: cut-down
 versions of console games from EA, Ubisoft, Activision and Sega. Excluded on
 prior-release grounds.
- **N-Gage Arena titles.** Nokia's online service closed in 2010. Several of the
 platform's most ambitious games depended on it and are now non-functional rather
 than merely unavailable.
- **The 2008 N-Gage service.** Nokia relaunched the brand as a download service for
 Symbian phones, then shut it down in 2010. Around 60 further titles existed there;
 they are mobile software and out of scope.

## Coverage gaps

- 8 of ~60 titles — the original-software layer. The conversion majority is excluded
 rather than uncatalogued.
- The 2008–10 service library is out of scope.
- Zero reissues, so these `Stranded` claims are stable.
