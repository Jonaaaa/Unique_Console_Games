## ⚠ A console with no console

Nuon was not hardware you bought — it was a **chipset licensed into other companies'
DVD players**, so "owning a Nuon" meant owning a specific Samsung or Toshiba model. VM
Labs went bankrupt in 2001 and the platform died with eight games released.

Every one is `Stranded`, and there is no rights holder: VM Labs' assets were liquidated
and the hardware partners never owned the software.

**Why Nuon strands games.** VM Labs' bankruptcy left no owner, the install base was
never even measurable because the chipset shipped inside third-party DVD players, and
the library is eight titles. The one escape happened because the developer retained his
own rights.
