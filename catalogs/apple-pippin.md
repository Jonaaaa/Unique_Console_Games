## ⚠ Apple's only games console

The Pippin was **Apple's licensed console platform**, built by Bandai around a PowerPC
Mac architecture with a CD-ROM drive and a built-in modem. It cost $599, arrived the
year before the PlayStation's price cuts, and sold roughly 42,000 units — fewer than any
other platform catalogued here.

Its library is largely Japanese edutainment and Mac CD-ROM conversions. Apple killed the
programme when Steve Jobs returned in 1997 and has never acknowledged it since.
