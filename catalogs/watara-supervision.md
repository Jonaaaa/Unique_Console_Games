## ⚠ A budget Game Boy competitor

The Supervision was a Taiwanese-manufactured monochrome handheld sold cheaply worldwide
under a dozen different brand names. Its library is ~70 cartridges of unlicensed
clones and small original games, mostly produced in-house or by anonymous Chinese and
Taiwanese studios.

Every title is `Stranded`, and the reason is documentary rather than legal: **nobody
knows who made most of them**. Developer credits are frequently absent, the publisher
was a hardware manufacturer rather than a games company, and Watara left the market.

**Why the Supervision strands games.** There is no rights holder to ask. Watara was a
hardware firm; its software was commissioned work with no surviving paperwork, and much
of the library is unlicensed clone material that could not be legitimately reissued
even if someone wanted to. `Ported` count of **zero**.
