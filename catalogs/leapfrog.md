## ⚠ Why the LeapFrog line shares one file

Leapster, Didj and LeapTV are distinct hardware with distinct media, but they form one
continuous educational product line from one company, with overlapping licensed content
and an identical stranding profile. Splitting them would produce three thin files that
repeat the same analysis.

They are included on the same basis as the [Sega Pico](catalogs/sega-pico.md) and
[V.Smile](vtech-vsmile.md): dedicated games hardware, removable media, a first-party
software catalogue. The `Notes` column identifies which system each title belongs to.

**Didj is the interesting one.** LeapFrog marketed it as a *games* handheld rather than a
toy, with real platformers built on licensed properties — the closest this category comes
to a conventional console.

cartridge systems. Every title needs a live licence re-cleared for an audience that has
