## ⚠ This file is short by rule, not by neglect

Three mechanics keep the Switch 2's debut list small, and all three are working as
the ruleset intends:

1. **Switch 2 Editions are not debuts.** `Tears of the Kingdom`, `Kirby and the
   Forgotten Land` and `Super Mario Party Jamboree` all have Switch 2 Editions, but
   those games debuted on Switch — they are catalogued there as `Ported`.
2. **Cross-gen sim-ships belong to both files.** `Metroid Prime 4: Beyond` and
   `Pokémon Legends: Z-A` launched on Switch and Switch 2 the same day, so they are
   `Sim-ship` here *and* in the [Switch](switch.md) catalogue.
3. **Third-party launch software is overwhelmingly ports.** `Cyberpunk 2077`,
   `Elden Ring`, `Street Fighter 6`, `Yakuza 0` — all older games arriving late.

What is left is a genuinely small set of Switch 2-only first-party titles.

**Why the Switch 2 strands games.** Same as the Switch: **policy**. Nintendo does
not license its software to competing hardware. Every entry below is stranded for
that reason alone, and none has any other obstacle.

One new wrinkle is worth recording early. The Switch 2 Joy-Con work as **optical
mice**, and `Drag x Drive` is built entirely around that — the first game in this
repo whose stranding cause exists from day one on current hardware.

## Coverage gaps

- **This is a one-year snapshot of a live platform.** Expect the list to grow and
  expect nothing here to move to `Ported` for a long time — Nintendo first-party
  software does not leave Nintendo hardware.
- Announced-but-unreleased titles are deliberately omitted. `The Duskbloods`
  (FromSoftware) and `Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave` will qualify on release.
- Third-party Switch 2 exclusives are thin so far and only spot-checked.
