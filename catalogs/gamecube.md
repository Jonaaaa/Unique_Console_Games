## ⚠ These entries will age faster than most

The Switch 2 GameCube library is **actively expanding**. Confirmed available:
`The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker`, `Soulcalibur II`, `F-Zero GX`,
`Luigi's Mansion`, `Wario World`, `Chibi-Robo!`, `Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance`,
`Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness`. Announced next: `Super Mario Sunshine`,
`Pokémon Colosseum`.

Every addition flips a row from `Stranded` to `Ported`. Re-verify before relying on
any `Stranded` claim for a Nintendo-published title here.

**Why the GameCube strands games.** For years the answer was
**nothing was being re-sold at all**: the GameCube was the only Nintendo home
console with no Virtual Console presence, skipped by Wii, Wii U and Switch alike.
That changed in June 2025.

What is left splits three ways:

1. **Rights tangles.** `Star Fox Adventures` was built by Rare, which Microsoft
 bought in 2002; Nintendo owns the IP, Microsoft owns the studio's output
 history. `Metal Gear Solid: The Twin Snakes` involves Konami, Nintendo and
 Silicon Knights, who sued Epic and lost catastrophically.
2. **Peripheral dependency.** `The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures` needs
 four Game Boy Advances and four link cables. `Donkey Konga` needs bongos.
3. **Competitive afterlife.** `Super Smash Bros. Melee` is the outlier, commercially
 valuable, legally unencumbered, and never re-released.

## Appendix: the tails

- **Licensed film and TV tie-ins (~120 titles).** The GameCube era overlapped the
 peak of the movie-licence platformer: Disney, Nickelodeon, Harry Potter, Lord of
 the Rings, Shrek. Almost entirely stranded on expired licences.
- **Sports annuals (~70 titles).** Madden, NBA Live, FIFA, NCAA, SSX. Stranded on
 league and athlete licences.
- **Japan-only retail (~50 titles).** Includes `Giftpia`, `Kururin Squash!` and
 several Namco and Bandai licensed titles.

## Coverage gaps

- The Switch 2 Nintendo Classics library is expanding; `Ported` is a floor and this
 file needs re-verification more often than the others.
- Licensed and sports tails characterised, not enumerated.
- Game Boy Player software is out of scope; it runs GBA cartridges, which is BC.
