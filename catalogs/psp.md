## ⚠ The PS Plus Premium problem

On the PSP, **`Stranded` status cannot be treated as settled**. Sony's PS Plus Premium Classics catalogue includes PSP titles that run
on PS4 and PS5 as distinct, downloadable store entries; which is a re-release on
another platform, not backward compatibility, so it flips a game to `Ported`. The
catalogue **rotates monthly**, Sony publishes no fixed list, and availability
varies by region.

Consequences:

- A `Stranded` entry here means *no known port, remaster, compilation or standing
 PS Plus Premium listing as of 2026-07-29*.
- Confirmed movers include `Tekken: Dark Resurrection`, `No Heroes Allowed!`,
 `Mr. Driller` and `Patapon 3` (added to PS4/PS5 on 18 Feb 2025).
- Any PSP `Stranded` claim should be re-checked against the current Premium catalogue
 before being relied on. This is a structural caveat, not a gap I can close.

**Why the PSP strands games.** Unlike the Wii U (hardware) and the Vita (studio
closures and litigation), the PSP's stranding cause is mostly **licensing and
Japanese-market gravity**. Its exclusives skew heavily toward anime tie-ins,
rhythm games with licensed tracklists, and Japan-only strategy RPGs, categories
where re-release rights are expensive or impossible to reassemble. Where a PSP
game was a wholly owned first-party or major-publisher property, it has usually
escaped: God of War, Metal Gear, Final Fantasy Tactics, Persona, Patapon, LocoRoco
and Kingdom Hearts all left the platform.

## Appendix: the Japanese licensed tail

The bulk of the 268-title `PlayStation Portable-only games` category is Japanese:
anime and light-novel tie-ins (`.hack//Link`, `Baka and Test`,
`A Certain Magical Index`, `Busou Shinki`, `Digimon Adventure`,
`Black Rock Shooter: The Game`), rhythm games with licensed tracklists
(the `DJMax Portable` line), Gundam strategy titles, and Japan-only strategy RPGs.

These share one stranding cause: **the licence, not the hardware**. A PSP anime
tie-in needs the anime rights, the music rights and the voice cast re-cleared
before it can be re-released, for a game with a small audience. That maths rarely
works, which is why this tail is the most reliably stranded part of the library
even though almost none of it has been individually verified here.

Full spine: [`Category:PlayStation Portable-only games`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:PlayStation_Portable-only_games) (268 entries).

## Coverage gaps

- **PS Plus Premium rotation.** See the [warning above](#-the-ps-plus-premium-problem).
 This is the largest source of uncertainty in the file.
- **Japanese tail.** ~180 of the 268 category entries are not individually verified
 and are excluded from the counts.
- **PSP Minis and Remasters.** The `PSP Remaster` line (PS3 ports of PSP games) is
 treated as a port, so those titles are marked `Ported`. PS Minis are excluded as
 multiplatform PSP/PS3 releases.
