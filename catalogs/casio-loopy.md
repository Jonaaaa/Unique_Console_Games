| **Coverage** | **Complete.** All 10 titles catalogued. | ## ⚠ A console with a built-in sticker printer

The Loopy was marketed exclusively to teenage girls in Japan and had a **thermal sticker
printer inside the console**. Its games exist largely to generate printable stickers of
your characters and screenshots, and a companion accessory let you capture video from a
TV and print that too.

Every title is `Stranded`, and the printer is why: the software's output medium is
physical adhesive paper. There is nothing to port the printing to.
